Classes

Each class has its roster and its own units. Drag assignments between units or into another class, and drag a unit’s name to move the unit. Drop a ManageBac ZIP on an assignment to open it there.

Saving and carrying

Everything above lives in this browser at this address. A linked folder keeps a second copy by itself. A take-home file carries all of it to another computer and back, protected by a passphrase.

How it works
  1. 1Mark. Pen, highlight, text, check marks, and comments that sit in the margin with a line to the spot. Arrange pages lets you combine a student’s files and drag pages into order.
  2. 2Score. Type a total, or click 0 to 4 on each rubric row (half points too). Or bring in your own rubric (Word, PDF or picture) once and grade right on each student’s copy. Students stay in their folder order.
  3. 3Enter grades. Open the Grades list and go down it in ManageBac. Click any score or comment to copy it. Marked in Kami instead? The Grade sheet finds what you typed, like “9 = 85%”, and shows a crop of it for every student. Files with no marks are left out as extra papers, and you can bring them back.
  4. 4Return. Export gives you the marked files in the same student folders.

Your marks, scores, units and rosters are kept in this browser, so you can close the tab and drop the same download in again to pick up where you left off.
