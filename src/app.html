Grading Studio

Open a ManageBac download, mark each paper, score it, and get everything back out in the same folders. Nothing goes anywhere except your own Google Drive, if you sign in.

Open a ManageBac download

The ZIP, or the unzipped folder with one folder per student.

Units

Drag assignments between units, and drag a unit’s name to move the unit. Drop a ManageBac ZIP on an assignment to open it there.

Rosters

Your class lists, kept in this browser (and your own Google Drive if you sign in). Used to put grades in roster order and spot who’s missing.
How it works
  1. 1Mark. Pen, highlight, text, check marks, and comments that sit in the margin with a line to the spot. Arrange pages lets you combine a student’s files and drag pages into order.
  2. 2Score. Click 4 to 1 on each rubric row (half points too). Students stay in their folder order.
  3. 3Enter grades. Open the Grades list and go down it in ManageBac. Click any score or comment to copy it. Marked in Kami instead? The Grade sheet finds what you typed, like “9 = 85%”, and shows a crop of it for every student. Files with no marks are left out as extra papers, and you can bring them back.
  4. 4Return. Export gives you the marked files in the same student folders.

Your marks, scores, units and rosters are kept in this browser, and synced through your own Google Drive when you sign in, so you can close the tab and drop the same download in again to pick up where you left off.
