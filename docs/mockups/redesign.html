Cleo Fixture: What the river keeps

The river in this story stands for time, and the narrator returns to it whenever the family is about to change. Each visit, the water has moved on while the stones stay where they were.

At first the narrator only sees the surface. By the last chapter she notices what the current has carried off, and what it has left behind on the bank.

The author never says this outright. The reader has to put the visits side by side.

That is why the ending feels earned, even though almost nothing happens in it.
